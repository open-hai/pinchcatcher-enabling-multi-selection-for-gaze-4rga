## Summary

Audited **PinchCatcher: Enabling Multi-selection for Gaze+Pinch** (CHI '25, `10.1145/3706598.3713530`) and built the reproduction repository at `/mnt/session/outputs`, mirrored in `/workspace/repro` and pushed to `origin/main` across three meaningful commits.

**Verdict: partial** — 4 of 18 inner-loop components reproduced, 12 partial, 2 blocked.

The substantive findings:

- **The blockers are missing specification, not difficulty.** SemiSwipe's activation distance — the confirmation criterion of the very technique the paper recommends — appears nowhere in the paper. FullDH assigns the same dominant-hand full pinch to both subselection and trial termination with no stated way to distinguish them, and taking §3.2's ungroup rule literally clears the group after every click. The 40-object grid is over-determined: the stated window at 13.5 m holds 112 lattice cells at the stated 1 m spacing, not 40.

- **Two results came out of the implementation rather than the reading.** A smoke test caught the FullDH ungroup collision as a live bug, which became hidden decision 13; and the unstated swipe distance turned out to be recoverable from the paper's own hand-movement means (0.060–0.162 m), with the estimator validated against the tilt threshold the paper *does* state — where its 2.67× overshoot independently confirms the overshoot §8 only claims qualitatively.

- **With no dataset released, the reported statistics could still be tested against each other.** 82 of 87 checks hold, including all 21 degrees-of-freedom pairs and all 21 partial η² values to printed precision. Five fail, and each is a discrepancy in the paper: two transposed §5.4 p-values, a Fig. 10 caption contradicting its own body text and means, a training-trial count off 4.5×, and an exclusion percentage that doesn't follow from its own count.

- **Open science scores 1 of 5.** Licence is CC BY 4.0, verified at three fetched endpoints. Code, data, preregistration and supplement are all "none found" after 26 recorded searches — and every negative is `none` rather than `dead`, since no artifact link was ever offered.
