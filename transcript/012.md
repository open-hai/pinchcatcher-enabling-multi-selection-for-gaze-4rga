## The boundary

The inner loop is five interaction state machines, a stimulus generator, five metric definitions and a declared statistical plan. I reimplemented all of it in `src/` and ran it: **19/19 mechanism tests pass** against every threshold the paper prints, and the analysis pipeline returns null on null data and recovers planted effects (709.5 ms/target vs planted 700). The outer loop — a 30-participant Quest Pro study — is untouched and unsimulated.

I deliberately kept the statistical pipeline and the paper's reported statistics *inner*: checking whether a paper's own numbers agree with each other needs arithmetic, not participants. That's where most of the findings came from.

## What blocks reproduction is specification, not difficulty

- **SemiSwipe's activation distance appears nowhere in the paper** — the confirmation criterion of the technique the paper recommends. I recovered it from the paper's own hand-movement means at 0.060–0.162 m, validating the estimator against the *tilt* threshold the paper does state (it overshoots 2.67×, which independently confirms the overshoot §8 claims qualitatively).
- **FullDH assigns the same DH full pinch to both subselection and trial termination**, with no stated way to distinguish them — and taking §3.2's ungroup rule literally clears the group after every click. My first implementation hit exactly that bug; a smoke test caught it, and it became hidden decision 13.
- **The 40-object grid is over-determined and inconsistent**: the stated window at 13.5 m is 15.0 × 6.0 m, holding 112 lattice cells at the stated 1 m spacing, not 40.

82 of 87 consistency checks hold — including all 21 df pairs (confirming N=30 and one sphericity correction) and all 21 partial η² values to printed precision.
